On the Record · how the checkpoint works

The AI keeps making things up.
The machine sorts the fakes out.

The search engine sprays out answers, and about half carry a fabricated or dead link — that never changes. The checkpoint fetches the page each answer cites and sorts: real evidence is delivered, fakes are caught. So among the answers that reach you, zero have a fake link — not because the AI improved, but because the fakes got caught.
