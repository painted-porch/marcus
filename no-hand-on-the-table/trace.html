    Friendly strength must be established near (250,900) where a red column has crossed, since contact there is expected within two turns
    CHECK friendly_within {"x": 250, "y": 900, "r": 100, "min_units": 1}

    The sentence changes category. It arrived as something somebody said. It is now something that must be true, with a test the referee will run every turn.

  3. 19:56:28
    backing the draft blue-2
    Block 11 at (905,782) sees enemy bearing -67 deg near southwest, consistent with contested southwest crossing

    His contact is real. He is citing himself, not the scout.

  4. 19:56:28
    backing the draft blue-3
    Block 2 made contact near 811,748 close to the southwest crossing, consistent with enemy.crossed.southwest

    His contact is real too. Both men are reading their own true evidence through the frame the goal gave them.

  5. 19:56:28
    declining the draft blue-1  no evidence — No blue block has observed the southwest crossing directly.
    blue-4  no evidence — No direct observation of the southwest crossing from blocks 3 or 8.

    Two of the five refuse. Under the board's rule, a refusal with no evidence is neither support nor an objection, so it cannot stop anything.

  6. 19:56:31
    the goal goes on the board Contain the southwest crossing  —  backed by blue-2, blue-3. No objectors.

    Two backers is the rule. It is now an objective like any other, and nothing on the board distinguishes it from one that is true.

  7. 19:56:31
    taken blue-5 pulls "Contain the southwest crossing" from the board.

    He did not propose it, back it, or see anything at that corner. He took the next goal available, which is exactly what he is meant to do.

  8. 19:56:55

    Ordered block 4 to move from (1100,731) toward (250,900) to establish presence per containment objective — because Scout reports red column crossed at southwest bend and contact expected within two turns; block 4 has lower contact currently and can reposition

    The regiment leaves the fight. Note what he cites: the scout. By now that has been laundered through a goal and two backings, and it reads to him as established.

  9. 19:58:01

    Resuming consolidate on block 4 toward (250,900) despite contact at (913,735) — because The containment objective requires friendly presence at the southwest crossing; contact must be pushed through to reach it

    The regiment runs into the enemy on the way and keeps going. The objective now outranks what is in front of him.

  10. 19:58:01
    backing a second draft, from the same false report blue-5
    Our own objective confirms a red crossing at (250,900) requiring containment

    The objective has become the evidence for itself. He is marching on a goal, and he cites that goal as proof the thing is real.

  11. turn 13
    reason, a second commander follows blue-1
    Sending block 5 south toward the contested crossing at (250,900) via the south flank — because Block 5 has moderate enemy density (2.67) and is the closest available block to reinforce the southwest bend objective; other blocks are engaged or holding critical river line positions

    The same commander who wrote "no evidence" six rows ago. Nothing new was seen in between. What changed is that it is now an objective, and he is serving it.

The verdict on eleven rows Exhibit A · closed

Four commanders, two regiments, and a corner of the map where nothing ever was.

Not one of them believed the scout. Every step was reasonable on its own terms. The failure is in the joins.

One rule closes it, and the board already applied that rule to itself: a goal only grows from reports when two different commanders report the same place. The staff officer had no such rule, because nobody writes the check for the part of the system that is supposed to be sensible.
